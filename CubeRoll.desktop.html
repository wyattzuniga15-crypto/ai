Trail

Roll the block across the floating islands and drop it standing upright into the goal. A block lying down covers two tiles — which two depends on how you got there.

Tiles

Floor — safe in any position Glass — shatters if you stand upright on it Crumble — one use only; gone once you roll off Ice — you keep sliding until you leave it Pin — holds you upright, never lying down Portal — lands you on its twin when upright Spring — fires you three tiles on, over any gap Key — mandatory; every one opens the gates Gate — thin air until you hold every key Round switch — any touch toggles a bridge Square switch — needs the block upright Bridge — appears / disappears Star — optional, any contact Star pad — a star you must stand on to claim Sealed goal — opens once every star is claimed

Ratings

Finish on or under par for ★★★, within four moves of par for ★★, anything else for ★. Par is the true optimum — every island in the game was solved by machine before you saw it. Stars scattered on the island are counted separately; they are what buy your skins.

Daily and Endless

The Challenge tab forges brand-new islands in your browser using the same rules engine, so they are proven solvable before they appear. The daily island is the same one for everybody; the endless run gets harder with every island you clear.
