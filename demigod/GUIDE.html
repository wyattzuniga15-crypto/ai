How it starts

Nobody hands you a book. This is the order it actually happens in.

Found

A satyr falls into step beside you and tells you not to panic. Four conversations over several minutes. He gives you two drachmas, then a bronze knife, and the only lesson that matters: ordinary metal will not touch them.

Claimed

Kill something that should not exist. A god speaks for you, everyone on the server hears it, and you are told your fatal flaw in the same breath. Big Three are 3% combined.

Armed

Forge celestial bronze. Until you do, monsters are invulnerable to you and you are not invulnerable to them. /chronoly help lists everything.

Hunted

Favour raises your scent, and scent brings worse things. Past a threshold, digging down stops working. Stand on gold, or inside camp's borders, and the world loses you.

Dead

You do not respawn. Your record is weighed and you land in Asphodel, Elysium or the Fields of Punishment. Leaving costs a drachma — /chronoly charon.

The eight parents

Press G or use /chronoly cast. Energy regenerates on your parent's terms — a child of Poseidon refills in water and barely at all in a desert.

Crafting

Every recipe in the build. Celestial bronze is the gate: almost nothing else matters until you have some.

The named eight

Summoned with /chronoly summon <name> (operator). Two of them cannot be beaten by damage at all. The five legendary relics are not craftable — each comes off the thing that guarded it.

Where it actually stands

  • Bosses wear vanilla bodies. The Minotaur is a ravager with 220 health, a name and a horn-break phase. Custom models are the next art pass; the fights are real now.
  • Nothing has been tested at runtime by its author. CI compiles and packages the mod but never launches a game. Every claim here is true of the code, not of a playthrough.
  • No guidebook in-game. The advancement tree is currently the only in-world thing pointing you at what to do next, which is why its descriptions carry instructions.
  • Abilities are one per parent. The design calls for four tiers each; tier one is what exists.
  • Camp and Olympus are built by command, not generated into the world.
